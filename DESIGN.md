## Brand & Style

This design system targets high-net-worth members, golf directors, estate concierges, and operational personnel at world-class golf sanctuaries and private country clubs. The brand personality balances old-world heritage with high-velocity modern enterprise efficiency. It feels discreet, quiet-luxury oriented, meticulously organized, and authoritative.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with subtle **Tactile Precision**:
- Generous, measured negative space reminiscent of heritage private member folios and bespoke horology journals.
- Crisp, hairline architectural partitioning paired with soft parchment underlays to eliminate the sterile coldness typical of generic enterprise SaaS.
- Micro-interactions that mirror physical luxury: micro-latches, weighted hover transitions, muted metallic accents, and razor-sharp typographic hierarchy.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of heritage fairway turf, limestone architecture, and brushed brass fixtures.

### Hierarchy & Functional Assignment
- **Primary Turf (`#16382C` & `#0F281E`)**: Anchor surfaces for primary navigation, high-level structural sidebars, dominant action buttons, and active member identities.
- **Warm Parchment Neutral (`#F9F9F6`)**: The primary canvas background. It softens eye strain compared to clinical white while delivering a warm, bespoke feel.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Strictly reserved for elevated workspace cards, active data table rows, modal overlays, and elevated popovers.
- **Muted Brass / Auric Accent (`#C5A880` & `#D4AF37`)**: Used sparingly for VIP status markers, active tee-time highlights, refined icon highlights, and luxury member tier designations.
- **Hairline Border (`#E5E7EB` & `#E8E6DF`)**: Subtle, low-contrast separation between administrative columns and data tables.

### Operational Status Palette
- **Confirmed / Paid (`Aceptada / Pagada`)**: Deep Emerald (`#0D5F3A` text, `#EBF7EE` surface, `#BCE7C7` border).
- **Pending (`Pendiente`)**: Antique Amber (`#92400E` text, `#FEF3C7` surface, `#FDE68A` border).
- **Checked-In (`Cliente Recibido`)**: Regatta Indigo (`#1E40AF` text, `#EEF2FF` surface, `#C7D2FE` border).
- **Completed (`Finalizada`)**: Slate Stone (`#475569` text, `#F1F5F9` surface, `#CBD5E1` border).
- **Canceled / Rejected (`Rechazada / Cancelada`)**: Crimson Rose (`#9F1239` text, `#FFE4E6` surface, `#FECDD3` border).

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses heritage prestige through an editorial display serif alongside high-legibility modern sans for high-density administrative workloads.

- **Headlines & Primary Metrics (`Playfair Display`)**: Used for page titles, luxury property and course designations, revenue summaries, and high-impact analytics. Adds timeless golf clubhouse sophistication.
- **Administrative Content & Data Grids (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric clarity and neutral precision across tee sheet schedules, booking tables, handicap indices, player rosters, and input forms.
- **Labels & Micro-data**: Always tracked out (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) and transformed to uppercase when indicating structural categories or status tiers, instilling an understated watchmaker-dial finish.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by a structured, responsive 12-column grid system running alongside an immutable 260px administrative command bar on desktop viewports.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Tee sheets leverage horizontal time-scrub lanes structured in 30-minute intervals.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout. Command bar collapses into an icon-first drawer. Data grids transition to card groupings with horizontal swipe for tee-time allocations.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) margins. Booking tables switch to sequential feed cards displaying time slot, member identity, hole assignment, and live badge status.
- **Rhythm**: Spacing follows a 4px modular matrix (`0.25rem`, `0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`). Generous vertical padding around table rows and cards ensures a calm, uncluttered executive workflow.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic heavy drop shadows, relying instead on **low-contrast architectural outlines** and **warm parchment tonal elevation**.

- **Canvas Base**: `#F9F9F6` establishes the physical desk substrate.
- **Resting Containers & Tables**: Pure `#FFFFFF` cards outlined with a subtle 1px border (`#E8E6DF`) with an ambient, diffused shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(22, 56, 44, 0.03), 0 6px 16px rgba(22, 56, 44, 0.02)`.
- **Hover & Interactive State**: Subtle translation (`-1px` Y-axis) paired with an enriched warm-tinted shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(22, 56, 44, 0.06)`.
- **Modals & Flyouts**: Elevated with a dark forest veil (`#0F281E` at 35% opacity with `4px` backdrop blur) and a crisp outline container shadow: `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(15, 40, 30, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a **Soft (Level 1)** corner philosophy. Sharp, crisp corners with a minute `4px` (`0.25rem`) radius on inputs and interactive elements, and `8px` (`0.5rem`) on primary content cards. 

This restrained geometry preserves the formal, bespoke stationery feel of luxury country clubs, avoiding overly playful or bulbous mobile-app conventions. Status pills and time-slot badges maintain a controlled, tailored pill-geometry (`9999px`) purely to contrast against rectangular table structures.

## Components

### Action Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Forest Green (`#16382C`) fill, Pure White text, 4px corner radius. High-polish hover shifting to `#0F281E` with subtle brass focus rings (`2px` solid `#C5A880` at 3px offset).
- **Secondary**: Parchment or White background with a 1px border in `#D4D0C5`, text in `#16382C`. Hover initiates a gentle ivory fill (`#F4F3EE`).
- **Tertiary / Auric**: Muted gold border and text (`#C5A880`) for private reserve amenities, VIP upgrades, and concierge actions.

### 30-Minute Time Slot Indicators
- Compact, high-precision badges (`padding: 4px 10px`, `label-font`, bold).
- **Available**: White background, 1px border `#E5E7EB`, text `#16382C`. Hover shifts to `#F9F9F6` with an `#C5A880` outline.
- **Booked / Occupied**: `#16382C` background, `#FFFFFF` text, paired with the player's handicap tier badge.
- **Reserved / VIP**: Soft brass wash (`#FBF7F0`), border `#C5A880`, text `#8A6D3B`.

### Status Badges
- Strict uppercase typography (`label-sm`), `4px` corner radius, `2px 8px` padding.
- Employs light pastel backgrounds with high-contrast, saturated text:
  - `Aceptada / Pagada`: Surface `#EBF7EE`, Text `#0D5F3A`.
  - `Pendiente`: Surface `#FEF3C7`, Text `#92400E`.
  - `Cliente Recibido`: Surface `#EEF2FF`, Text `#1E40AF`.
  - `Finalizada`: Surface `#F1F5F9`, Text `#475569`.
  - `Rechazada / Cancelada`: Surface `#FFE4E6`, Text `#9F1239`.

### Data Tables & Tee Sheets
- **Headers**: Clean horizontal ruling with `#E5E7EB`, upper-case tracked font (`11px`, `#6B7280`), parchment background (`#F9F9F6`).
- **Row Anatomy**: Generous vertical breathing room (`padding: 16px 20px`), background `#FFFFFF`, bottom border `#F1F0EC`. Row hover produces an ultra-soft `#FBFBFA` warm highlight.
- **Player Details**: Dual-line presentation with Member Name (`title-md`, `#16382C`) over Member ID and Handicap Index (`body-md`, `#6B7280`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px hairline border (`#D1D5DB`), `4px` radius. Focus shifts border to `#16382C` with a faint brass aura (`0 0 0 1px #C5A880`).
- Checkboxes and radios feature custom Forest Green active states with brass tick marks.
